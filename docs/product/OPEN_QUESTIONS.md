## Applying the decisions

The 10 MB limit belongs to TestPilot's upload policy. Do not inject it into cases generated for a different uploaded PRD whose limits are unknown. Likewise, an unknown target-system rule still requires Need Confirmation even though this project's original Open Questions are resolved.

Preserve approved PRD/Business Flow text. Cite the original section plus the applicable resolved OQ when a clarification matters. Implementation choices must stay within this baseline; any genuinely new product blocker should be documented with evidence rather than silently answered.
